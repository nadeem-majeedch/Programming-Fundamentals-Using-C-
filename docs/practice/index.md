# Practice Bank — 200+ problems

> **The rule of the bank:** every problem is a complete item — statement, difficulty, topics, input/output, constraints, sample tests, hints, reference solution, explanation — and every problem is *pedagogically distinct*: no variable-renamed duplicates. If two problems look alike, their *idea* differs (that difference is stated in the explanation).

## How to work the bank

1. **Match the tier to your units, then pick by topic weakness** — the matrix tells you where your ticks are thin.
2. **Attempt on paper or in the editor for 15 minutes before any hint.** The hints are graded; take exactly one.
3. **Test with the samples before inventing more.** Then invent the edge case the samples *don't* cover — that habit is the real syllabus.
4. **Read the reference solution only after your own works** — then compare *designs*: what did yours compute that the reference doesn't, and vice versa?
5. **Tick the checklist cell only when the problem passes its own samples plus one self-invented test.**
