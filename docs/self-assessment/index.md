# Self-Assessment Hub

> **The system's one rule:** attempt *everything on the page* before opening any
> answer. Every answer key here is hidden in a collapsible section (or a separate
> page, where the course's unit quizzes live) precisely so a stray glance can't
> destroy the self-test. Reading the key *is* the lesson — but only after your
> own attempt.

## What's in the system

| Collection | Count | Purpose | When to take it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[Weekly quizzes](weeklies-1.md)** | 16 × 10 questions | verify the week's two sessions stuck | end of each study week |
| **[Topic revision tests](topic-tests-1.md)** | 8 × 12 questions | consolidate a *topic* that spans units | after finishing each topic cluster |

**Total: 34 assessment instruments · 366 questions · every one with difficulty label, answer, and explanation.**

## The three question families

- **Recognition** (multiple choice, true/false) — fast, but the wrong options
  are real confusions students actually have, not filler.
- **Prediction & tracing** (output prediction, algorithm tracing, dry runs) —
  the executable-mental-model questions; the heart of the system.
- **Diagnosis & construction** (debugging, code reading, short programming) —
  you find the bug, name what a snippet does, or write 5–15 lines from a spec.

## How to take any instrument

1. **Closed book first** — no compiler, no notes, one sitting, 15–40 minutes
   depending on the instrument.
2. **Write answers down** — paper or a file; "I knew that" doesn't count.
3. **Open the key once**, item by item, and for each miss write one sentence:
   *what I confused it with*. That sentence is the study plan.
4. **Score with the bands** (below) and record the result in the checklist.
5. **Retake rule:** a retake counts only after you've re-studied the flagged
   sections *and* slept on it. Same-day retakes measure memory, not learning.

## Scoring bands (course-wide)

| Score | Verdict | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **≥ 90%** | Ready | Move on; note any lucky guesses for the retake bank. |
| **70–89%** | Almost | Re-study the sections your misses point to; retake after a day. |
| **< 70%** | Not yet | Redo the unit's exercises and revision sheet; retake when fresh. |

**Progress rule (unchanged from [Assessment](../assessment.md)):** a unit is
complete when its lab works and its quiz is ≥ 90%.
